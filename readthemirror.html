Read the Mirror

Nobody has ever read a strand of DNA by looking at it. Every sequencing machine ever built does something stranger: it copies the strand and writes down what the copier says. Which means what comes out is the mirror image — and you have to turn it back. Copy the Code is the machinery being eavesdropped on here.
