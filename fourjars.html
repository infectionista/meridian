Four Jars, One Tree

Four mystery organisms are glowing in jars on the lab bench: a bacterium, a virus and two parasites. By the end of this session you will decide who is actually alive, catch your own eyes lying to you, watch DNA typos tick like a clock, count your way to a real family tree — and find out which of these creatures is your relative.
