Build the Protein

A gene is a string of letters. A protein is a chain of beads. Nothing in the letters looks anything like a bead, and yet every cell in your body does the translation thousands of times a second without ever making a dictionary. Here is the dictionary, and here is what happens when one letter of it goes wrong. Copy the Code is where the DNA came from.
