Flip the Switch

Every cell in your body carries the same two metres of DNA, and almost none of it is being used. A liver cell and a neuron are not different because they hold different genes — they hold exactly the same ones. They are different because of which ones are reachable. This is where genetics stops being about what you have and starts being about what is switched on. Aim the Scissors is how you get at a switch.
